Do I need an account?

No. MotivWall works on-device without an account. Your goals, quotes, and images stay on your device.

How do I set the wallpaper on iOS?

Export the image to Photos, then set it as your Lock/Home screen from the Photos app.

How do I set the wallpaper on Android?

Use the Save & Set as wallpaper action inside MotivWall (Photos/Media permission required).

What’s included in Premium (one-time)?

Unlimited projects and saved templates, advanced layout options (more rows/columns, larger blocks), and 4K export.

Do you read my data?

No. We don’t read or collect your project content. MotivWall is local-first; your data stays on your device. Purchases are validated by Apple/Google (via RevenueCat) with minimal, anonymized identifiers.

How do I report a bug or request a feature?

Email support@motivwall.app with your app version, OS version, and device model. For bugs, include steps to reproduce; for features, explain the problem and the impact.
